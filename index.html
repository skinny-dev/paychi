How it works

Request, review, quote, payment

Every request is checked by a person before anything is paid. You decide whether to go ahead after you see the quote.

  1. 1

    Request

    Send us a payment link, an invoice, or a link to the item you want to buy, with any details we need to know.

  2. 2

    Review

    Our team checks that the request is clear and that we're able to complete it for your location and the service involved.

  3. 3

    Quote

    We send you a quote for that specific request. Nothing is charged at this point, and you can decline.

  4. 4

    Payment

    If you accept, you pay the quoted amount and we complete the payment with the service on your behalf.

What to send

Three kinds of requests

Payment link

A checkout or payment page generated by the service you want to pay.

Invoice

An invoice issued to you that needs to be paid internationally.

Purchase link

A link to a product, plan, gift card, or in-game item you want bought for you.

Good to know

What to expect

A person reviews each request

Requests aren't paid automatically. We check each one first and may ask you questions.

You see the quote first

Each quote is specific to your request. You only pay if you choose to accept it.

Not every request is possible

Availability depends on your location, the service, and applicable requirements. If we can't help, we'll tell you.

FAQ

Questions

How much does it cost?

Each request gets its own quote, which you see before paying anything. If the quote doesn't work for you, you can decline.

How long does it take?

It depends on the service and the request. We'll let you know what to expect when we send your quote.

Can you pay for any service from any country?

No. Availability depends on your location, the service, and applicable requirements. We confirm whether we can help during review, and we decline requests we're unable to complete.

What should I include in my request?

The payment link, invoice, or purchase link, plus anything that helps us complete it correctly — for example the plan, region, or amount you need.
