Google

Google subscriptions and purchases.

For example Google One storage or purchases on Google Play. Send the link to what you want and the Google account details we need.

What to send us

  • The link to the plan, product, or checkout page
  • Which plan or item you want
  • The region your Google account is set to, if you know it

What you get

Payment for what you chose, plus the confirmation we receive. Your quote explains how the payment is applied to your account.

How this request works

  1. 1

    Request

    Send us a payment link, an invoice, or a link to the item you want, with any details we need.

  2. 2

    Review

    Our team checks the request and whether we’re able to complete it for your location and the service.

  3. 3

    Quote

    We send you a quote for that specific request. Nothing is charged, and you can decline.

  4. 4

    Payment

    If you accept, you pay the quoted amount and we complete the payment with the service on your behalf.
