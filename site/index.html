How it works

Request, review, quote, payment

Every request is checked by a person before anything is paid. You decide whether to go ahead after you see the quote.

  1. 1

    Request

    Send us a payment link, an invoice, or a link to the item you want, with any details we need.

  2. 2

    Review

    Our team checks the request and whether we’re able to complete it for your location and the service.

  3. 3

    Quote

    We send you a quote for that specific request. Nothing is charged, and you can decline.

  4. 4

    Payment

    If you accept, you pay the quoted amount and we complete the payment with the service on your behalf.

What to send

Three kinds of requests

Payment link

A checkout or payment page generated by the service you want to pay.

Invoice

An invoice issued to you that needs to be paid internationally.

Purchase link

A link to a product, plan, gift card, or in-game item you want bought for you.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost?

Each request gets its own quote, which you see before paying anything. If the quote doesn’t work for you, you can decline.

Does submitting a request mean I have to pay?

No. You only pay if you accept the quote.

Can you pay for any service from any country?

No. Availability depends on your location, the service, and applicable requirements. We confirm during review whether we can help, and we decline requests we’re unable to complete.
