FAQ

Questions and answers

If your question isn’t here, add it as a note when you submit a request and we’ll reply.

General

What does Paychi do?

You send us a payment link, invoice, or purchase link. We review it, send you a quote, and if you accept and pay, we complete the payment on your behalf.

Who reviews my request?

A person on our team. Requests aren’t paid automatically, and we may ask you questions before quoting.

Can you pay for any service from any country?

No. Availability depends on your location, the service, and applicable requirements. We confirm during review whether we can help, and we decline requests we’re unable to complete.

Requests

What should I include in my request?

The payment link, invoice, or purchase link, plus anything that helps us complete it correctly: the plan, amount, region, or account details.

My service isn’t listed. Can I still ask?

Yes. The services on this site are common examples. Send the link and we’ll tell you whether it’s something we can do.

How do I check on my request?

Use the reference number you get after submitting on the Track a request page. We’ll also contact you if we need anything.

Quotes and payment

How much does it cost?

Each request gets its own quote, which you see before paying anything. If the quote doesn’t work for you, you can decline.

Does submitting a request mean I have to pay?

No. You only pay if you accept the quote.

How long does it take?

It depends on the service and the request. We’ll tell you what to expect when we send your quote.

Have a link you need paid?

Send it to us for review. Submitting a request doesn’t commit you to paying.
