How it works

From link to completed payment

Every request goes through the same four steps. A person reviews it, you see a quote, and nothing is paid until you accept.

  1. 1

    Request

    You send us what you want paid.

    You

    • A payment link, invoice, or purchase link
    • Details like the plan, amount, region, or account
    • How we can contact you

    Paychi

    • Receives the request and gives it a reference number
  2. 2

    Review

    We check the request before anything else happens.

    You

    • Answer any questions we send you

    Paychi

    • Checks the link, item, and price shown by the service
    • Checks whether we can complete it for your location and the service
    • Asks you if anything is unclear
  3. 3

    Quote

    You see what this request will cost before paying.

    You

    • Read the quote
    • Accept it, or decline with no charge

    Paychi

    • Sends a quote specific to your request
  4. 4

    Payment

    Once you pay, we complete the purchase.

    You

    • Pay the quoted amount using an option we offer you

    Paychi

    • Confirms your payment
    • Pays the service on your behalf
    • Sends you the confirmation, code, or instructions

Good to know

What to expect

A person reviews each request

Requests aren’t paid automatically. We check each one first and may ask you questions.

You see the quote first

Each quote is specific to your request. If you decline it, the request closes and you aren’t charged.

Not every request is possible

Availability depends on your location, the service, and applicable requirements. If we can’t help, we’ll tell you instead of sending a quote.

Have a link you need paid?

Send it to us for review. Submitting a request doesn’t commit you to paying.
